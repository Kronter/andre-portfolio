## Project overview

Helping Hands is a stealth comedy about a misunderstood raccoon trying to fix up its town. We built it for Global Game Jam 2020, inspired by the theme **Repair**.

I led the overall Game Design, joined a three-person programming team to shape the stealth mechanics, and created cel-shading and snow effects that matched our art direction. My focus was always on making the experience feel satisfying and cohesive.
