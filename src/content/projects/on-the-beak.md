## Project overview

On The Beak is a fast-paced arena brawler about wrestling penguins trying to stay on the iceberg and grab the most fish. We made it for Kiwijam 2020, inspired by the theme **On the brink**, and it ended up tying for first place overall.

I led the game design, jumped into general gameplay programming alongside my team, and built the custom iceberg and water shaders to support the game's look and feel.
